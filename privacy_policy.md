# ProofCam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This policy explains how the ProofCam app ("ProofCam", "the app"), published by [DEVELOPER OR COMPANY NAME] ("we"), handles your information.

## Summary
- No account or sign-up.
- Your photos stay on your phone. We do not upload them.
- Your location is used to stamp photos. To show an address and a map, coordinates are sent to a geocoding service and a map tile provider.
- The free app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data.

## Information the app uses

**Camera.** Used to take photos. Photos are saved on your device (in the app and in Pictures › ProofCam). We don't receive or store them.

**Location.** Used to show coordinates, address, map and time on your photos and to write GPS data into the photo's EXIF metadata. To turn coordinates into an address, the app uses your phone's built-in geocoding service (provided by Google on most Android phones). To draw the small map, the app requests map tiles for your area from MapTiler (or OpenStreetMap). These requests reveal your approximate area to those providers. We don't store your location on any server.

**Photo storage.** The app saves photos to your phone's gallery. On Android 10 and below it asks for storage permission to do this.

**Settings.** Your stamp settings and the list of photos taken in the app are stored only on your device.

## Advertising
The free version shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and information about ad interactions and app performance, to show ads and measure them. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Sharing
We don't sell your data. Data leaves your device only as described above (geocoding, map tiles, AdMob), or when you choose to share a photo with another app.

## Retention and deletion
Everything ProofCam stores is on your device. Delete photos in the app, or uninstall the app to remove its data. Copies saved to your gallery remain until you delete them there.

## Children
ProofCam is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Security
Network requests made by the app use HTTPS.

We'll update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the date above.
